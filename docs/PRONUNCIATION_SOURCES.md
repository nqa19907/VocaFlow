2026-10-16 against the Cambridge Dictionary pronunciation entries below. The
existing original definitions, sentences, and translations are unchanged.

Cambridge's raised optional schwa is displayed as a regular explicit `ə` for
legibility in `significant`, `evidence`, `sustainable`, and `plausible`. This
normalizes typography and selects the pronounced-schwa variant; it does not
introduce a different sound. `contrast` is a noun in the current example, so its
stress is on the first syllable. Syllable dots and length marks follow Cambridge's
notation.

Korean romanization and Mandarin pinyin already present in the starter content are
preserved. Written readings do not depend on browser speech synthesis; synthesized
audio depends on the voices installed on the device and can vary by accent.
